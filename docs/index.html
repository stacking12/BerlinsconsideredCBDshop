FAQs

Questions we hear at the counter.

Is CBD legal to buy in Germany?+
Yes. CBD products sold at AnyWeed are compliant with German and EU regulations. If you're ever unsure about a specific product's THC content or legal status, just ask our staff — they can walk you through it.
What's the difference between your CBD and cannabis products?+
CBD products are non-intoxicating and legal for general sale, while cannabis products may contain THC and are sold under the applicable rules for adult access. We clearly label every item so you know exactly what you're choosing.
Do I need to be a certain age to shop at AnyWeed?+
Yes, you must be 18 or older to purchase cannabis and CBD products in our store. We may ask for ID at the counter, so please bring one along.
Do you sell pre-rolled joints and rolling accessories?+
Yes — we stock pre-rolled CBD and hemp joints, plus rolling papers, filters, grinders and other smoking accessories for anyone who prefers to roll their own.
Where is AnyWeed located and what are your hours?+
You'll find us at Kastanienallee 14, 12627 Berlin. Check our Google listing for current opening hours, or give us a call at +49 163 2648031 before you head over.
